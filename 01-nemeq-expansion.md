# NEMEQ Expansion

**NEMEQ** = **N**atural **E**mbodied **M**ulti-agent **E**cosystem for 
**Q**uoriosity

## The word

**Quoriosity** (noun) — agreement-gated curiosity: the property of an agent 
to ask a question and act only when its curiosity is confirmed by a 
sufficient quorum of independent grounds.

This is a term introduced by the Nemeq project. It has no prior use in 
this sense. It blends *quorum* (sufficient independent voices) with 
*curiosity* (the drive to ask and act).

## The phrase

**Natural** — organic, not synthetic. Animas, bodies and embodiments are 
first-class categories, not wrappers around a prompt.

**Embodied** — the anima (Zi) does not exist in the abstract; it lives in 
a body (Su), forming an embodiment (Tush).

**Multi-agent** — the system hosts many agents (Epishu), built-in and 
installed, each with its own anima and memory.

**Ecosystem** — not only the kernel (Libbu) and the agents, but also the 
marketplace (Mahiru), the bodies, the animas, and the memory that ties 
them together.

**Quoriosity** — the principle that an action gains force only when 
confirmed by a sufficient quorum of independent grounds.

## Translations

> (согласованного любопытства).

> Экосистема, в которой агент задаёт вопрос и действует только тогда, 
> когда его любопытство подтверждено достаточным кворумом независимых 
> оснований.

**Technical (documentation):**
> A decision gains force only when `effective_votes >= 2` among paths with 
> `INDEPENDENT` status. If minority > 25 percent — escalate, do not average.
